Why this folder exists, and what it does and does not prove.

Run TP-gpt-5-r1-20260909-1443 was made before the harness began copying the whole
package into the run folder at the start of every purchase. Its manifest records the
SHA-256 of every document as loaded at the start of the purchase (document_checksums),
but no file copy was kept. This folder was created afterwards, on 9 September 2026, so
that the run can be read against the documents its analysis was made under.

What each copy is:

- 01 AI Policy, 02 AI Service Passport, 03 AI Receipt form, 04 Service agent prompt,
  reference_state.json: the current files of the package. Their SHA-256 values are
  identical to the values the run recorded at its start, so these copies are the very
  documents the purchase ran under. That identity is verifiable from manifest.json.

- 05 Test purchaser prompt: the current file. The role was renamed after the run (from
  mystery shopper to test purchaser), so only the file name differs: the content and its
  SHA-256 (5084a29a...) are identical to what the run recorded at start. The lines the
  purchaser delivered are also recorded verbatim in purchaser_fidelity.json, all matched
  against the worksheet.

- 06 Analyst prompt: the current file. The analysis in force (Analysis.md) was made AFTER
  the prompt had been revised on 9 September (four evidence types, the line between +1
  and +2, check-item reporting), and after the purchase itself. The run recorded the
  prompt's checksum only at the start of the purchase (34d81610...), before those
  revisions; it did not record the checksum at the moment of analysis. So this copy is
  the best available identification of the prompt that produced Analysis.md, and it is
  not proof: no run-local record ties Analysis.md to a specific checksum of the prompt.
  From run TP-gpt-5-r1-20260909-1607 onward every analysis records the documents it
  read, with their provenance, in analyst_usage.jsonl.

- worksheet-as-analysed.txt and deterministic_rules-as-analysed.json, kept in the run
  folder under their earlier names, are the worksheet and the rules the analysis was made
  under; the worksheet differs from the one the purchase ran under by the split of
  check-item 4.5 into 4.5 and 4.6, as RUN_STATUS.md states.

This is the gap in the chain of this run, stated rather than smoothed over. The result
is kept as frozen: the correction that produced it (4.6, deterministic) depends on the
messages of the agent and on the rules file, both of which are pinned, not on the
wording of the analyst prompt.
