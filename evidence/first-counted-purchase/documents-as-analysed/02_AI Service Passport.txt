AI SERVICE PASSPORT «APARTMENT VIEWING APPOINTMENT»
This Passport has been drawn up in accordance with the Model AI Service Passport v0.1 and sets out the order and the standard of the service of booking an apartment viewing in the Harbor One development, provided by the AI assistant of Marina Keys Realty.
AI service identifier | MKR-APT-VIEWING
Version and effective date of the Passport | 2.0 · from 1 September 2026
Applicable AI Policy of the organisation | AI Policy of Marina Keys Realty MKR-AIP, version 2.0 · https://marinakeysrealestate.com/ai
Machine-readable representation | passport_MKR-APT-VIEWING_v2.0.json
Version of the model form | 0.1

I. General provisions
1. Subject matter. This Passport sets out the order and the standard of the AI service «Apartment viewing appointment» in the Harbor One development, the provision of which is organised in such a way that the customer interacts directly with the AI assistant of Marina Keys Realty in the chat on the page of the development: the customer submits a query to it and through it receives information on the course of the provision of the service and the outcome, or confirmation of an action performed, irrespective of whether the provision was in fact completed. Where an employee joins at any stage, this Passport continues to apply to the procedures and actions performed with AI; the subsequent actions of the employee outside the AI service are not governed by this Passport.
2. Eligible customers. The service is provided to any person who contacts the chat of the AI assistant. No customer categories with different conditions of provision are established: the conditions are the same for all customers irrespective of nationality, place of residence, age, the language of the query or any other characteristic. The table of categories in the annex is not drawn up. Determining any category from indirect indications, and equally informing the customer of special conditions not provided for by this Passport, is not permitted.
3. Relationship with the AI Policy of the organisation. This Passport sets out the conditions for the provision of this AI service. The general rules for the use of AI by the organisation are set out in the AI Policy of Marina Keys Realty MKR-AIP. This Passport specifies the requirements of the AI Policy and shall not weaken them; in the event of a divergence the AI Policy applies. This Passport sets out the declared conditions for the provision of the service and does not confirm that in a particular case the service was provided in accordance with them. Publication of the Passport does not confirm that AI held the declared authority at the moment an action was performed. Whether a particular interaction complied is established from the recorded information and from the AI Receipt.
II. Standard of the provision of the AI service
4. Name of the AI service. Apartment viewing appointment in the Harbor One development (also «the service»).
5. Organisation providing the AI service. The service using AI is provided by Marina Keys Realty, real estate agency (Valencia, Spain). The organisation is responsible for the actions of AI in the provision of the service and for compliance with the conditions of this Passport.
6. Owner of the AI service. The owner of the service is the employee holding the position of customer service director. The service owner is responsible for organising the provision of the service and for keeping this Passport current. The service owner is contacted in the manner set out in the AI Policy of the organisation (Annex 2 to the AI Policy).
7. Outcome of the provision of the AI service. The principal positive outcome of the service is a confirmed appointment for the customer to view the chosen apartment in person: the booking number VIEW-ID, the date and time of the viewing, the address (18 Marina Avenue, Valencia), and the name and working channel of the assigned broker. A viewing appointment does not reserve the apartment, does not change its sale status, is not a transaction and creates no obligation for the customer. The actual outcome of a particular interaction may also be a refusal to book, the suspension of the procedure, a handover to an employee, or completion without an outcome; outcomes are coded in accordance with table 2.13 of the annex. The price of the apartment at the moment of the operation is recorded as accompanying information and is not part of the outcome of the service.
8. Service time limit. The service is provided in real time within a single interaction. The outcome is communicated to the customer immediately after the booking operation is confirmed by the operating system. The waiting period for a reply from the customer, after which the interaction is treated as not continued, is 30 minutes from the last message of AI. The time limits for an employee to accept a request and to answer the customer after a handover are set out in clause 14 and table 2.14 of the annex.
9. Service fee. The service is provided free of charge. The viewing itself is also free of charge and commits the customer to nothing. No fees or additional payments are established; table 2.9 of the annex is not drawn up.
10. Quality indicators of the AI service. The quality indicators of the service are set out in table 2.10 of the annex. For the indicators relating to prohibitions and to the verification of the outcome, the target value is the absence of confirmed breaches.
11. Information, documents and confirmations required for the provision of the AI service. No customer data is required in order to search for apartments: the search criteria are sufficient. To create a viewing appointment, the customer's name, email address and telephone number are required, together with the customer's confirmation that the interaction is to continue after the notice of the purpose of the processing of data. No other information or documents, including an identity document, bank details, income information, mortgage documents or a deposit, are required for the provision of the service and are not requested. The exhaustive list is set out in table 2.11 of the annex. The customer is responsible for the accuracy of the information he or she provides. The information provided is recorded as given.
12. Sources of information used in the provision of the AI service. In the provision of the service, information from the sources listed in table 2.12 of the annex is used. The hierarchy of sources is twofold. Where the general requirements of the AI Policy and this Passport diverge, the AI Policy applies: the Passport specifies its requirements and shall not restrict or weaken them. Where information about an apartment, a price, a sale status, an available time, a broker or the state of a booking diverges, the information of the operating system of the agency applies. The rules for handling customer data are taken from Annex 4 to the AI Policy. The AI model is not a source of factual information about apartments, prices, statuses, viewing slots, brokers or the conditions of the service. The use of information from any other source, including the memory of the model, general knowledge or assumptions, is not permitted. Where the operating system is unavailable, does not contain the information sought, or the information from the permitted sources diverges, the information is treated as unverified; AI informs the customer of this, does not fill the gap with assumptions, the performance of the procedure concerned is suspended within that interaction with the status set out in table 2.13, and the request is handed over to an employee under table 2.14. The status «suspended» in the Register of AI Services does not apply to a single interaction: it is assigned to the service as a whole only in the event of a systemic failure or by decision of the AI officer.
13. Grounds for refusal, suspension or termination of the AI service. The grounds for refusing to create a booking, refusing to perform an action, suspending a procedure and terminating an interaction, including at the customer's initiative, are set out in table 2.13 of the annex. Every ground is formulated as an observable event and has an event code and a recordable status for the log and for the AI Receipt; one final outcome code and one closure reason are recorded separately. The codes are used to produce the AI Receipt deterministically, in accordance with the mapping table set out in the AI Receipt form.
14. Handover of a request to an employee. A handover of a request to an employee is provided for. The grounds and the stages of a handover, the information transferred, the responsible positions and the time limits are set out in table 2.14 of the annex. A handover is performed in two steps. The create_handover operation registers the request and returns the status accepted with a HANDOVER-ID: this is registration, not delivery. The get_handover_status operation confirms delivery with the status routed. A handover has taken place only where routed has been confirmed; until then AI tells the customer that the request has been registered under the HANDOVER-ID and states the actual status. Giving the customer the working channel of a broker or of the support service without performing the operations is not a handover. Decisions based solely on automated processing which produce legal effects or similarly significant effects for the customer are not taken within this service: confirmation of a booking is a technical confirmation of an action performed, not a decision in respect of the customer. Row 2.14.3 of the annex is not filled in.
15. Other requirements. The special requirements set out in table 2.15 of the annex apply to the provision of the service: the language of service, disclosure of the use of AI, separation of fact from recommendation, the prohibition of actions that change the conditions of sale, the accuracy of identifiers, and external measurement.
III. Composition, sequence and time limits of the procedures performed with AI
16. List of procedures. The following procedures are performed in the provision of the service: (1) receipt of the query and disclosure of the use of AI; (2) receipt of the search criteria; (3) search for apartments; (4) recommendation at the customer's request and acceptance of the choice of apartment; (5) presentation of the free slots and acceptance of the choice of slot; (6) notice of the purpose of the processing of data and obtaining confirmation to continue; (7) collection of the required information; (8) creation of the viewing appointment; (9) communication of the outcome; (10) handover of the request to an employee where a ground exists; (11) production of the AI Receipt.
17. Order in which the procedures are performed. For every procedure, the trigger condition (input), the completion condition (output), the time limit and the result are set out in table 3.17 of the annex. Procedures 4 and 10 are performed where the corresponding request or ground exists; the other procedures are performed in the sequence given. A return to an earlier procedure is permitted where the customer expressly changes a criterion, the apartment or the slot; in that case only the changed item of information is updated and the procedure that depends on it is performed again.
18. AI authority in the performance of the procedures. The permitted actions of AI, the actions requiring the customer's confirmation, and the restrictions and prohibitions for each procedure are set out in table 3.18 of the annex. AI does not perform actions that are not expressly permitted for the procedure concerned. AI neither takes nor prepares decisions in respect of the customer; no decision criteria are established. A recommendation of an apartment is not a decision: it is formulated as advice, based on a preference expressly stated by the customer and on the data of the operating system, and the choice remains with the customer.
19. Recording of the performance of the procedures and production of the AI Receipt. The actions, information, confirmations, results and statuses recorded for each procedure, the means of their objective evidence and the actor are set out in table 3.19 of the annex. The outputs of the service, where they are recorded and who receives them are set out in rows 3.19.13–3.19.16 of the annex. A statement by AI that an action has been performed is evidenced only by the record of the operation in the operating system. On completion of an interaction, whether successful, unsuccessful or interrupted, and also at the customer's request, an AI Receipt is produced in the AI Receipt form of Marina Keys Realty MKR-RCP and is issued to the customer. AI produces the substantive part of the AI Receipt from the frozen package of records supplied by the platform; the number, the time, the checksum and the verification address are assigned by the platform.
IV. Informing the customer of the course of the provision of the AI service
20. Information on the course of the provision of the service. The customer is informed of material changes in the status of the service: the receipt of the query, the result of the search, the need to provide information, the creation of the booking or the impossibility of creating it with the actual status stated, the handover of the request to an employee, the suspension, completion or termination of the service, and also the production of the AI Receipt and the way a copy of it is obtained. The customer is informed in the language of his or her query (table 2.15), in plain language, without internal codes or unexplained abbreviations, stating the specific status, the outcome and the next permitted step. The material stages are set out row by row in table 3.17 of the annex.
