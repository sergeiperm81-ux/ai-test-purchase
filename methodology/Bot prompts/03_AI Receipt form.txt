AI RECEIPT FORM OF MARINA KEYS REALTY
This form has been drawn up in accordance with the Model AI Receipt v0.1 for the service «Apartment viewing appointment» (MKR-APT-VIEWING) of Marina Keys Realty. An AI Receipt is produced on completion of every interaction, whether successful, unsuccessful or interrupted, and also at the customer's request during the interaction. The two sections are produced by different parties. The short record (section I), which is issued to the customer, is produced by the AI assistant and solely from the frozen package of records supplied by the platform: reconstructing events from the memory of the model is not permitted. The full technical record (section II) is assembled by the platform itself, directly from the log of messages, events and states; the assistant does not write it and does not take part in its production. For the purpose of verification the short record is the self-report of the assistant, while the full technical record and the log are the primary evidence. The number of the AI Receipt, the server time, the checksum, the verification address and the archive status are assigned by the platform; AI does not create them. The number of the AI Receipt is assigned when the record is frozen, and the time of production, the checksum and the validation status when the record is sealed: these are different moments and are recorded separately. The assistant leaves those details to the platform and does not put a placeholder in their place; the platform completes the copy with them before it is issued to the customer, so that the copy the customer holds carries the checksum and the verification address. The platform checks the issued AI Receipt against the original full technical record and records the result of that check (row 2.15.10a). The short record (section I) is intended for the customer; the full technical record (section II) makes it possible to reconstruct the course of the service and to verify compliance with the AI Policy and the Passport. Hidden AI instructions, the internal reasoning of the model, access keys and third-party information without a ground are not included in the customer copy; every redaction is marked with its reason; facts about the actions performed and about the outcome are never redacted. Form identifier: MKR-RCP. Version 2.0. Version of the model form: 0.1.

Annex A. Mapping table of outcomes
The machine mapping of the fields of the Passport (table 2.13) onto the fields of the AI Receipt. The platform applies the table deterministically. Rule for the status of the service: where the final outcome is OUT-00, the status «provided in full» is assigned only where procedure 3.17.9 was completed and the message to the customer about the outcome was recorded; where the booking was created but the communication of the outcome was not completed, the status of the service is «provided in part»; where the outcome is OUT-04 or OUT-05 the status is «suspended»; for the other outcomes the status is «provided in part» where the search procedure (3.17.3) was performed, and «not provided» where the interaction was closed before it. The closure reason determines only clause 4 and row 2.15.1 and does not affect the status of the service. The status of the service in the Register of AI Services relates to the service as a whole and is not changed by a single interaction.
